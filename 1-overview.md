Eczema is a chronic skin condition in which the physical and emotional effects can significantly impact everyday life. Keeping up with eczema care can quickly become overwhelming. With risks of infection or Topical Steroid Withdrawals, it is highly important to take care of your overall well-being.

This app simplifies eczema management by **tracking symptoms and flare-ups**, along with a _**mood tracker**_ to monitor well-being. Users can log meals and skincare products in a _**food & skincare diary**_ to spot potential irritants. _**Medication and treatment reminders**_  appear as daily tasks on the home page, rewarding users upon completion.

To help prevent flare-ups, _**smart environmental alerts**_ notify users of temperature or pollen changes. When symptoms become severe, an _**SOS first-aid kit**_ provides guidance for managing emergencies such as bleeding or infections. Users can access _**educational resources**_ and generate a _**doctor’s PDF report**_ for consultations.

To encourage healthy habits, the app features progress _**streaks**_ for regular use. _**Analytical insights**_ help users better understand their eczema over time.

This will be useful to all those struggling with eczema. Children and people with additional care needs often struggle more.
